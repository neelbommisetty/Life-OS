# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Neel, using his own mobile-friendly personal software environment. The initial scenario discussed is opening Life-OS on his phone in the morning to understand his situation and direction across life areas.

## Product Purpose

Life-OS offers connected, specialized experiences over shared personal information. Codex initially executes the existing Life vault workflows and publishes structured records for the application. Providers remain replaceable over time. The current planning authority is [Current Vision](docs/vision/VISION.md); the legacy planning set is historical context.

Every homepage helps Neel understand where he was, where he is, and where he wants to be. Both measurable evidence and subjective experience belong in that account. Success means seeing his own trajectory and noticing meaningful progress without losing difficult or uncertain parts of the story.

## Brand Personality

Personal, candid, encouraging. These words summarize the confirmed direction: show Neel's actual context, keep uncertainty visible, and offer encouragement grounded in evidence. The finalized ocean-blue visual identity for all Life-OS web slices is documented in [Life-OS design system](docs/DESIGN.md): near-white working surfaces, pale blue navigation, cool gray text, and ocean-blue accents with light blue primary controls.

## Anti-references

The current vision excludes a generic notes dashboard, a new agent dashboard or chat interface, and isolated app silos. Do not force feelings into numeric scores, invent progress, conflate suggestions with commitments, or turn every intention into a task.

## Design Principles

- Organize home experiences around past context, current situation, and desired direction.
- Let current priorities determine the featured evidence and presentation; the first dataset does not permanently define the homepage.
- Pair the reflective perspective with an operating view of actual tasks and calendar commitments; shifting focus does not hide obligations.
- Give subjective experience and objective evidence appropriate forms of expression.
- Ground encouragement in real changes, including mixed outcomes.
- Share records and relationships across experiences, with sources and freshness available.
- Start with a useful connected slice and let specialized views evolve through use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-friendly use is confirmed. No particular accessibility accommodation or formal conformance target has been specified in this conversation. Future web slices follow the shared [design system](docs/DESIGN.md), including its responsive and legibility guidance. Following the first content sketch, Neel authorized an interactive demonstration of how the same history can be presented differently as priorities change.
